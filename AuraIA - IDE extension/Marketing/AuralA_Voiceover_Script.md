### 🎬 Technical Appendix — Sync Guide

For a 4-minute runtime:  
- Read each line in 2–3 seconds, with natural pacing and micro-pauses.  
- `[pause]` indicates a 1–1.5 second silent breath for emotional impact.  
- Use soft ambient music (70–80 BPM) with gradual build toward the final minute.  
- Match slide or visual transitions approximately every 10–15 seconds.  
- Fade to logo reveal during final line, music volume reducing by 30% over last 5 seconds.  

Recommended tools:  
- **ElevenLabs** or **Play.ht** for narration.  
- **DaVinci Resolve**, **Premiere Pro**, or **Final Cut Pro** for alignment and fade timing.
